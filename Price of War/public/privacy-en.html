PRICE OF WAR

Community rules and privacy policy · updated October 2026

The game is in testing, made by a single person. This page explains, plainly, what the game stores about you and what is expected of players.

Community rules

  • Respect your opponent: no insults, harassment, hate speech, or threats, in names, quick messages, or the way you play.
  • Don't use an offensive name or one that pretends to be someone else. The game automatically refuses some names.
  • Don't cheat: no tampering with the rules, no deliberately exploiting flaws, no using several accounts to fix results or asking other accounts for points.
  • Anyone who crosses the line may be suspended from online play. In a match, Options → Report opponent alerts the creator.

What the game stores

  • Account: you sign in with Google, Discord, email and password, or as a guest. We store the account identifier and, when you sign in with email, Google, or Discord, its email. Passwords are not kept by the game: they stay with the sign-in service (Supabase), protected.
  • Profile and progress: player name, avatar, level, XP, crowns, cards, and decks.
  • Matches: the moves and result of each online match, points, and ranking position, so the game works and so reports can be checked.
  • Presence: the time you last opened the game and the days you played, only so the creator knows how many people play.
  • Reports and suspensions: who reported whom, the reason, and the short text the person wrote.
  • On your device: settings, volume, and a copy of your progress.

The game has no ads, uses no third-party trackers, and doesn't sell data. Your player name and ranking are visible to other players; your email is never shown to them.

Where the data lives

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the account and match data on Supabase (a cloud database service). The game's creator, being the administrator, can see account data, including the email, but only uses it to keep the game running, handle reports, and give support.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Delete my account. This erases the profile, cards, decks, points, and history tied to the account, forever. It can't be done in the middle of a match. If you can't, talk to the creator through the same place where you found the game.

If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account with email.

Changes

The game is still changing. If this page changes in an important way, the date at the top changes. Questions or requests about your data: talk to the creator through the same place where you found the game.

This text is an honest summary of what the game does today, not legal advice.
